Normat

Normat is a long-running Norwegian mathematical journal and newsletter that publishes articles, expositions and information relevant to the Norwegian mathematical community. This page summarizes the Normat website and provides direct links to the official online archive.

About

Normat (Nordisk matematisk tidsskrift / Normat) has historically functioned as a forum for mathematical exposition, announcements and community news. The official site maintains an archive of past issues and article contents. For the complete, original material please visit the source (linked below).

Official archive and issue index

Normat's public issue index and archive are available here:

  • Normat — official website
  • Normat — Innhold (issue index / archive) — directory listing of per-year issue pages and PDFs

Selected historical notes

Normat has connections to the older Nordisk matematisk tidsskrift; some historical material and older year-collections are referenced from the Normat pages. The archive contains listings and downloadable files for many years (e.g., 2003–2011 and older collections).
