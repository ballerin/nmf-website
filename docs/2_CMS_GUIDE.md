# CMS Guide — Editing from your browser

The CMS (Content Management System) lets you add and edit content through a web form. No coding, no installs.

## Opening the CMS

1. Go to **https://nmf-website.pages.dev/admin/**
2. Click **Login with GitHub**
3. Authorize the app (first time only)
4. You'll see the dashboard with three sections: **News**, **Events**, **INFOMAT**

## Adding a news article

1. Click **News** in the left sidebar
2. Click **New News** at the top
3. Fill in:
   - **Title** — the headline
   - **Date** — publication date
   - **Author** — your name or "NMF" (optional)
   - **Image** — click to upload a hero image (optional)
   - **Body** — the article text. Use the toolbar for formatting (bold, links, headings)
4. The right pane shows a live **preview** of how it will look
5. Click **Publish** at the top → **Publish now**
6. Wait 1-2 minutes for the site to update

## Adding an event

1. Click **Events** → **New Events**
2. Fill in:
   - **Title** — event name
   - **Date** — publication date
   - **Start Date** / **End Date** — when the event happens
   - **Body** — description, program, registration info
3. Click **Publish**

## Adding an INFOMAT issue

1. Click **INFOMAT** → **New INFOMAT**
2. Fill in:
   - **Title** — "INFOMAT — [Month] [Year]" (e.g. "INFOMAT — June 2026")
   - **Date** — issue date
   - **Author** — editor name (e.g. "Arne B. Sletsjøe")
   - **Body** — the issue content (calendar, articles, news)
3. Click **Publish**

## Editing existing content

1. Click a collection (News, Events, or INFOMAT)
2. Click on any entry in the list
3. Make your changes
4. Click **Publish**

## Deleting content

1. Open the entry you want to remove
2. Click the three dots (•••) at the top
3. Click **Delete**
4. Confirm

## Images

- Click the image field to upload from your computer
- Images are stored in `assets/images/uploads/`
- For the best result, use landscape images around 1200×600 pixels

## Tips

- **Save often** — the CMS auto-saves drafts, but publish when you're done
- **Preview** — always check the right pane before publishing
- **Wait** — after publishing, it takes 1-2 minutes before the change appears on the live site
