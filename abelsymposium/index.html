About the symposium

The Abel Symposium brings together leading mathematicians for a focused program of invited lectures, mini-courses and panel discussions. It complements other national mathematical activities and often features topics closely related to the Abel Prize and current research trends.

Typical programme elements:

  • Invited plenary lectures by internationally recognised researchers
  • Short talks and poster sessions for early-career researchers
  • Special sessions on recent developments bridging pure and applied mathematics

Audience: researchers, postdocs, PhD and master students, and professionals interested in current mathematical research.
