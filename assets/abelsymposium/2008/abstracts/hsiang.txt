Wu-Yi Hsiang - "On the critical optimality of for mixture sphere packings of two sizes".

Let SP=SP' \union SP'' be the non-overlapping union of infinite packings SP', SP'' of
unit spheres (resp. r-spheres, r<1) and p(r) be the optimal density all such packings
of (1,r)-type, which is a function of 0<r<1. 

We shall call its critical points (resp. values) the critical radii (resp. their corresponding 
critical optimalities) of such packings. 

We shall prove that r=\sqrt{2}-1 is the most outstanding critical radius 
whose corresponding critical optimality 
   p(r)=4\pi/3*(1+(\sqrt{2}-1)^3)/(4*\sqrt{2})=0.793104817...
is the maximum of p(r) over a rather large interval:

Theorem I. p(r) has a unique maximum at r=\sqrt{2}-1 over the interval [0.3,1) with
the critical optimality given by the above formula.

Theorem II. The limiting optimal infinite packing of spheres with (1,\sqrt{2}-1) radii
are given by those hexagonal close packings of unit spheres with all of their big-holes
filled by (\sqrt{2}-1)-spheres.
