Sergei Merkulov - "Wheeled props, deformation theory and quantization".

The theory of operads and props is nowadays much more than just a useful technical tool.
It has grown into a kind of universal mathematical language with the help of which topologist, algebraists, 
homotopy theorists  and  geometers can fruitfully communicate with each other.
We shall discuss some key ideas of  the theory of (wheeled) props, and focus on its recent applications  
to the deformation theory of algebraic and geometric structures,  quantization of Poisson manifolds, 
and the  Batalin-Vilkovisky formalism.
