Alexander Verbovetsky (joint with P.Kersten, J.Krasil'shchik and R.Vitolo) - "Hamiltonian structures for general PDEs".

We will discuss the following question: What happens to a Hamiltonian operator of an evolution system 
if we change coordinates so that the system becomes non-evolution?

We will give an answer to this question, describe the corresponding geometry, consider examples, in particular, 
construct a bihamiltonian structure for Kupershmidt's nonholonomic deformation of a bihamiltonian system.
