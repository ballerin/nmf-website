Most of the selected restaurants are in the middle price-range, which i Oslo means main courses starting at a little less that NOK 200. (Yes, eating out in Oslo is expensive!) An asterisk (*) indicates somewhat less expensive places, with prices mostly between NOK 100 and 200.

  • Mauds (Norwegian), Tollbugata 24, phone 2242 0118
  • Serpico (Italian, pizza, but no pasta) Solligt. 2, phone 2313 1145

Vegetarian

It may be better to choose vegetarian dishes at an Asian or Italian restaurant, but here are two purely vegetarian options (both cafeteria style):
  • Vegeta verstshus, Munkedamsveien 3b,
  • Krishna's cuisine, Kirkeveien 59 B
