Each contribution must adhere to the standards given by Springer-Verlags instructions:

  • [PDF, 12 pages] Instructions for authors
Important: In order to make changes after the referee-process, please ensure that you are editing a copy of your tex-files you have received from Håvard Berland. Manual editing of the tex-files is strictly necessary to be able to compile all contributions to one file.

More details with examples and the necessary style-files may be found on the webpage:

  • Springer - For Authors, LaTeX2e Macro Packages

The deadline for contributions has been extended from 1 March to the summer of 2005. The contributions may be sent to any of the members in the editorial committee, Ola Bratteli, Sergey Neshveyev and Christian Skau, preferably with a cc to Havard.Berland(at)math.ntnu.no

When a paper has been accepted for publication and is in its final form, please send the LaTeX-file by e-mail to Havard.Berland(at)math.ntnu.no.

Un-refereed contributions may be found here.

Note: Please do not zip the LaTeX-files (or other files) you send, because over 99% of all zip-files sent via e-mail contain viruses and will therefore be deleted by our mail server.
