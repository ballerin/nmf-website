The participants will be lodged in this conference center, and all lectures and meals will take place there.

The lectures takes place in the lecture room "Einar Gerhardsen". This room is equipped with overhead projectors for transparencies, white boards and a data/videoprojector (People wanting to use the last alternative must bring their own laptop)

Just outside the lecture room is a coffee bar where the participants may make coffee and tea at all times. During the two long coffee breaks and after dinner coffee will be served in the main lounge.

All meals take place in the restaurant.

The participants have to pay themselves for drinks purchased in the bar and taken from the mini-bars in the rooms, and also for phone calls from the room, when checking out. All standard cards are accepted.
