Participants of this Abel Symposium are expected to receive 3 Circular Letters on 19.03, 28.04 and 30.05. They contain some important practical information, which won't be repeated here. If you lack one of the letters, contact us.

The mathematical part of the meeting is not bounded to formal presentations, but the participants are invited to informal discussions, especially because not everybody will deliver a lecture. We'll book a special room for this purpose. The social program consists of an excursion to Sommarøy -- an island on the border of the Arctic Ocean. Informal social activity can be easily composed via guidelines of Destination Tromsø.
