Proceedings of the Abel Symposium 2008 will be published by Springer-Verlag in the special series Abel Symposia.

We invite submissions from both the speakers and the regular participants. All submissions will be refereed, but we intend to move quickly towards publication. The deadline for contributions is set to be October 1, 2008.

Each contribution must keep the standards given by Springer-Verlags:

  • Here you will find the macros (use the link above For contributed books, proceedings, and similar (individual files) if you don't want zipped files).

For those who are not familiar with the styles: Basically you need to write at the beginning of the paper \documentclass{svmult} and take care that the style-file svmult.cls belongs to the same directory, where your TeX file lives. Read more in the Instructions for authors.

Please submit electronically both TeX and PDF files of your manuscript to one of the editors (or with reply to Abel Symposium 2008):

Boris Kruglikov, Valentin Lychagin, Eldar Straume.
