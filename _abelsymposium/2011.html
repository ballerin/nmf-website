The central theme of the symposium was representation theory of quivers and algebras and related topics. The representation theory of quivers has had an increasing impact in mathematics, especially due to its concrete and combinatorial nature. It has influenced and has connections with many other areas of mathematics, like commutative algebra, algebraic geometry, algebraic groups, and combinatorics. The symposium focused on several of these connections like cluster algebras, homological and representation theoretic aspects in commutative algebra and quiver varieties.
