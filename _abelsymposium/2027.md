The symposium brings together mathematicians and theoretical physicists working where operator algebras, quantum information theory and mathematical physics meet: operator-algebraic methods for quantum correlations, nonlocal games and quantum channels, and the new questions in operator theory that quantum information has raised.

The symposium takes place at the Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters (Det Norske Videnskaps-Akademi) in Oslo.

Updated information can be found at [the symposium's home page](https://www.mn.uio.no/math/english/research/groups/operator-algebras/events/conferences/abelsymposium-2027.html) at the University of Oslo.
