Venue
NTNU - Norwegian University of Science and Technology
Trondheim

The scientific program centered around geometric questions in Several Complex Variables and Complex Dynamics, including holomorphic laminations/foliations, pluripotential theory, the \(\bar\partial\)-equation, CR-geometry, Function Theory and Kaehler Geometry. The aim of the Abel symposium is to present the state of the art of the theory and related topics, and to discuss future research directions.

The organizing committee consisted of John Erik Fornæss (NTNU), Marius Irgens (NTNU), Yum-Tong Siu (Harvard), Erlend F. Wold (Oslo) and Shing-Tung Yau (Harvard).
