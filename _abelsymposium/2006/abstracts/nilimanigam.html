--- layout: abelsymposium_template title: "Nilimanigam" year: 2006 kind: sub old_url: "https://abelsymposium.no/symp2006/abstracts/NilimaNigam" excerpt_separator: "" --- {% raw %} We experience the interaction of waves with obstacles several times in our daily lives; the waves could be light, sound, pressure or elastic waves, to name but a few possibilities. The study of these interactions has long fascinated mankind, and led to some very interesting mathematical developments. In this talk, we shall survey some of the history behind computational scattering. We shall then focus on some central challenges which remain in the field, and describe some promising approaches to their resolution. {% endraw %}
