Flying to Ålesund

In the evening of Wednesday May 24 the participants of the Abel Symposium 2006 are expected to arrive at Ålesund Hotel SAS Radisson .

Some flight schedules and prices can be found here. For a complete list, see www.sasbraathens.no.

Getting from the airport to Ålesund

The airport bus service is synchronized with arriving and departing flights. Take the bus to the town center. The current fare is NOK 75, and the bus takes 25 minutes.

Another possibility is to go by taxi, the fare is ca NOK 380 (not reimbursable).

Hotel reservation and payment

If your name is on the list of participants , and you have emailed your arrival and departure information to me before April 20, you will have a reservation in the hotel. On departure you pay only for extra costs like accompanying persons, telephone, bar tabs etc.
