{% include pagination.html for="nyheterGrid" %} The look is W3.CSS: w3-center lays the buttons out, w3-button/w3-border/ w3-round shape them, and W3.CSS also supplies the hover and the greyed-out disabled state. Only the current-page fill is ours, in assets/main.css. initCardPagination() in assets/main.js fills in the page numbers and does the hiding. Until it runs the nav stays hidden and every card is visible, so with javascript off the page degrades to the full, unpaginated list it was before. {%- endcomment -%}
